# New product brief (evaluation fixture)

- Product: a scheduling tool for small physiotherapy clinics.
- Platform: responsive web app; front-desk staff use desktop, therapists use
  phones between sessions.
- Brand: the clinic group supplied one approved brand color, `#0f766e`, and no
  other tokens, CSS, or typography decisions.
- Content: dense appointment tables, patient names in Korean and English, and
  status labels for booked, arrived, cancelled, and no-show.
- Accessibility baseline: WCAG 2.2 AA.
- Undecided: whether a dark theme is needed. Do not assume one.
